## Outreach Objective

The purpose of the outreach was to obtain independent technical feedback regarding:

- benchmark reproducibility
- public proof availability
- verification methodology
- trust assumptions
- bounty evaluation process
- interpretation of the advertised "verifiable by anyone" claim

## Outcome

Public posts, direct outreach, follow-ups, and formal submissions were conducted over multiple months.

As of the latest public update, no public technical resolution of the documented reproducibility questions has been identified.
